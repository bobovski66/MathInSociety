Week 7 Laboratory — Cost, Revenue, and Profit

Write linear business models, calculate break-even points, compare profit at different sales levels, and interpret marginal profit.

Before you begin

  1. Translate the story into fixed cost \(F\), variable cost \(v\), price \(p\), and quantity \(q\).
  2. Write cost, revenue, and profit functions before substituting a production level.
  3. Treat break-even as the boundary between loss and profit, then use marginal profit to describe one additional unit.

Key notation

\(C(q)=F+vq\), \(R(q)=pq\), and \(\pi(q)=R(q)-C(q)=(p-v)q-F\). Break-even is \(q^*=F/(p-v)\) when \(p>v\). Marginal profit is \(p-v\).

Models used this week
Cost: C(q) = F + vq    Revenue: R(q) = pq
Profit: π(q) = R(q) − C(q) = (p − v)q − F    Break-even: q* = F/(p − v)

F = fixed cost, v = variable cost per unit, p = selling price per unit, q = number of units.
