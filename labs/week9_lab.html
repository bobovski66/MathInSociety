Week 9 Laboratory — Networks and Collective Systems

Analyze node degree, paths, connectedness, diffusion, and bottlenecks in a small network.

Before you begin

Use the edge list as the source of truth. A path counts edges; degree counts incident edges. For spread, count only direct neighbors unless a question says otherwise.

Network: edges AB, AC, AD, BE, CE, DF

Part 1 · Degree

Find the degree of A and E.

Part 2 · Paths

Give the number of edges in A–D–F and A–B–E–C.

Part 3 · Connectedness

Is every node reachable from A? How many nodes are in the network?

Part 4 · One-step diffusion

If only A initially has a message, how many new nodes receive it after one step?

Part 5 · Bottlenecks

Which edge is a bridge whose removal disconnects F from the rest?
