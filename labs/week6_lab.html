Week 6 Laboratory — Interest and Loans

Calculate simple interest, compound growth, compounding frequency, doubling time, and month-by-month loan balances.

Before you begin

  1. Identify the principal, rate, time, and compounding period before choosing a formula.
  2. Calculate at least one growth or loan period by hand so you can see what the percentage is applied to.
  3. For a loan, separate each payment into interest and principal reduction before comparing payoff plans.

Key notation

Simple interest: \(A=P(1+rt)\). Compound interest: \(A=P(1+r/n)^{nt}\). Rule of 72: doubling time \(\approx72/r_{\%}\). Loan update: \(B_{new}=B(1+r/12)-M\).
