Week 5 Laboratory — Binary, Encoding, and Information

Practice positional notation, binary conversion, exact and approximate binary fractions, compression, parity, and information measured in bits.

Before you begin

  1. Write place values before converting a number between base ten and base two.
  2. For compression and parity, write the actual encoded or transmitted string, not only a verbal description.
  3. For information, connect the probability to a power of \(1/2\) whenever possible.

Key notation

Binary place values are powers of \(2\). Even parity makes the total number of 1s even. Information is \(I=-\log_2(p)\); if \(p=1/2^k\), then the outcome carries \(k\) bits.
