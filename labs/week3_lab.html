Week 3 Laboratory — Center and Spread

Compute measures of center and spread, see how an extreme value changes them, and use standard deviation to compare consistency.

Before you begin

  1. Compute the center before the spread so every deviation has a clear reference point.
  2. For standard deviation, show deviations, squared deviations, division by \(n\), and the final square root.
  3. When two data sets have similar centers, use spread to decide which is more consistent.

Key notation

Mean: \(\bar x=\frac1n\sum x_i\). Range: maximum minus minimum. In this course, standard deviation is \(s=\sqrt{\frac1n\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2}\).
