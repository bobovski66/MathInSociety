Week 8 Laboratory — Convex Geometry

Classify convex sets, graph linear inequalities, test feasible points, find vertices, and evaluate linear expressions at corner points.

Before you begin

  1. Replace each inequality by its boundary equation before deciding which side is feasible.
  2. Test a point in every constraint; feasible means satisfying all constraints at the same time.
  3. Find every vertex before evaluating an objective expression at the corners.

Key notation

A linear inequality defines a half-plane. The feasible region is the intersection of all required half-planes. A convex set contains the entire line segment joining any two of its points.
