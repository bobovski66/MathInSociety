Week 4 Laboratory — Normal Distributions and Inference

Use the Empirical Rule and z-scores for individual values, then use samples and confidence intervals to make statements about populations.

Before you begin

  1. Decide whether the question concerns one individual value or a population estimate.
  2. For an individual value, use the Empirical Rule or a z-score; for a population estimate, use the sample statistic and margin of error.
  3. State interval endpoints in the original units and check the comparison value against the entire interval.

Key notation

\(z=(x-\mu)/\sigma\), so \(x=\mu+z\sigma\). An estimate with margin \(E\) gives \([\text{estimate}-E,\text{estimate}+E]\). For similar studies, \(E\propto1/\sqrt n\).
