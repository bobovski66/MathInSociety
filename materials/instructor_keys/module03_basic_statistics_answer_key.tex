\modulekey{3}{Basic Statistics}
\textbf{Prequiz}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For \(2,3,2,4,9\):
\[
\bar x=\frac{20}{5}=\boxed4,
\]
ordered data are \(2,2,3,4,9\), so median \(=\boxed3\), mode \(=\boxed2\), and range \(=9-2=\boxed7\).
\item Removing \(9\) leaves \(2,2,3,4\):
\[
\bar x=\frac{11}{4}=\boxed{2.75},\qquad \text{median}=\frac{2+3}{2}=\boxed{2.5}.
\]
The mean changed by \(1.25\), while the median changed by \(0.5\), so the \textbf{mean changed more}.
\item Five students with mean \(80\) have total score
\[
5(80)=400.
\]
The four known scores total \(72+78+85+81=316\), so the missing score is
\[
400-316=\boxed{84}.
\]
\item Mean \(=5\). Squared deviations:
\[
(3-5)^2=4,\quad (5-5)^2=0,\quad (5-5)^2=0,\quad (7-5)^2=4.
\]
Their sum is \(8\), the mean squared deviation is \(8/4=2\), and
\[
s=\sqrt2\approx\boxed{1.41}.
\]
\item Machine A range: \(252-248=\boxed4\). Machine B range: \(256-244=\boxed{12}\). Machine A is more consistent because its values have less spread.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Matching quiz}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For \(6,7,6,8,13\): mean \(=40/5=\boxed8\), median \(=\boxed7\), mode \(=\boxed6\), range \(=13-6=\boxed7\).
\item Removing \(13\) leaves \(6,6,7,8\): mean \(=27/4=\boxed{6.75}\), median \(=(6+7)/2=\boxed{6.5}\). The mean changes more.
\item Six scores with mean \(75\) total \(450\). The five known scores total \(370\), so the missing score is \(\boxed{80}\).
\item Mean \(=4\). Squared deviations are \(4,0,0,4\), so
\[
s=\sqrt{\frac84}=\sqrt2\approx\boxed{1.41}.
\]
\item Set A is more consistent. Both sets have mean \(50\), but A stays within \(49\) to \(51\) while B ranges from \(30\) to \(70\).
\end{enumerate}
