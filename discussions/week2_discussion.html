Who gets asked?

What can a small survey tell us, and whose answers might be missing?

The situation

At a community event, 20 people answer the question, “Would you attend an evening workshop?” Twelve answer yes.

The fraction who answer yes describes the people surveyed. To interpret it for a larger community, consider how those 20 people were selected.

Your post

Use the survey above to calculate a percentage and think about the people represented.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. What fraction and percentage of the 20 respondents said yes? Show the calculation.
  2. Imagine that the survey was given only to people already attending a community event. Who might be missing, and how could you reach them?
  3. Would these responses be enough to plan the workshop? Explain what else you would want to know.

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
