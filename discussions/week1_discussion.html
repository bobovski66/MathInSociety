A small change, repeated

Where do repeated changes appear in everyday life?

An example

Someone practices for 10 minutes on Monday, then adds 5 minutes each day. The next three practice times are 15, 20, and 25 minutes. The practice times form the sequence 10, 15, 20, 25.

We see sequences like this in savings, game scores, and everyday routines.

Your post

Choose a number you might keep track of: minutes of practice, items collected, points in a game, or something else that matters to you. A real or made-up example is fine.

Answer the four questions using your example, then post your response in Canvas.

  1. What are you keeping track of, and why does it matter to you?
  2. Choose a starting number and one change to repeat, such as adding 5 each day or taking away 2 each round. Show the next three numbers. What does one step mean in your example?
  3. Would a fraction or a negative number make sense in your example? Give a reason.
  4. How long could your pattern reasonably continue? What might make it change?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate's example and respond with a question, a suggestion, or a connection to your own experience.

You might start with:

  • “What would happen if you changed by ___ each time instead?”
  • “Would half of a ___ make sense here? I am thinking about…”

Include a sentence explaining your question or suggestion.
