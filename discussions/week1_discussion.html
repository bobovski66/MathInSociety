A small change, repeated

How can a simple number rule describe change in everyday life?

The situation

A score starts at 10 points and increases by 5 points each round. The rule is to add 5 to the current score after every round.

A sequence records the starting amount and each new value. You can imagine a different setting where a similar rule might apply.

Your post

Use this number pattern and imagine a situation it could describe.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Starting at 10, calculate the scores after rounds 1, 2, and 3. Show the sequence, including the starting value.
  2. What might these numbers represent? In your situation, would a fractional or negative value make sense? Explain.
  3. How long might adding 5 each round remain reasonable? What could cause the rule to change?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
