Use the logistic map to investigate sensitive dependence on initial conditions.

Context

The problem

Consider the deterministic recursion \(x_{n+1}=3.9x_n(1-x_n)\). Choose one pair of nearby initial conditions:
Pair A
0.2000 and 0.2001
Pair B
0.2500 and 0.2501
Pair C
0.3500 and 0.3501
Use a calculator, spreadsheet, or the Module 10 explorer. Keep at least six decimal places during the iteration.
Initial post

What to include

  1. Create a table showing both trajectories and their absolute difference for at least the first 10 generations.
  2. Continue until the absolute difference first exceeds 0.05, or through generation 15 if it has not yet done so. Report that generation.
  3. The two starting values differ by only 0.0001. Explain what the later separation says about long-range prediction in this model.
  4. Explain why this behavior does not mean the recursion is random: both trajectories still follow exactly the same deterministic rule.
  5. Comment briefly on why rounding too aggressively during the calculation can change the observed trajectory.
Replies
