How far would you trust a prediction?

How far should we trust a prediction from a repeated rule?

The situation

Start with 5 and use the update rule “double the current number, then add 1.” Compare it with the same rule starting at 6.

The starting amounts differ by 1. Both sequences follow exactly the same arithmetic rule.

Your post

Calculate both sequences and think about predictions made from measured starting values.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Calculate three updates from each starting amount. How far apart are the two results after the third update?
  2. Imagine the rule models a changing quantity. What might the difference between these predictions mean?
  3. What observations or changing conditions would help you decide how far ahead to trust either forecast?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
