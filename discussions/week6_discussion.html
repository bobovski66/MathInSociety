A payment that works for you

How does your monthly payment affect the loan balance and your other priorities?

The situation

Imagine someone borrowed $500 for an expense of their choice. The loan charges 1% interest each month. They can make a monthly payment of either $30 or $75.

Interest is added before each payment. Use ending balance = starting balance + interest − payment. Assume the interest rate stays fixed and there are no additional charges.

Your post

Use the amounts above. You can invent the borrower’s circumstances to explain their choice. Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Calculate the first month’s interest on $500. Then calculate the remaining balance after a $30 payment and after a $75 payment. Show your arithmetic.
  2. Imagine a reason for borrowing the money. Which payment would you choose for that situation, and what other expenses or priorities would influence the decision?
  3. Which choice leads to less interest in the second month? Explain using the balances you calculated. What might make the borrower reconsider their choice?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask what might change their decision, or suggest another factor they could consider.
