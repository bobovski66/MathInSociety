Would it be worth offering?

What would make a small sale or service worth your time?

CostProfitWhat matters

Start with something familiar

Imagine selling 10 handmade items for $5 each. Getting started costs $20, and materials cost $2 per item. Money collected is 10 × $5 = $50. Total cost is $20 + 10 × $2 = $40. Profit is $50 − $40 = $10.

That $10 is what remains after the costs counted here. Your time or other costs could change whether the idea feels worthwhile.

Your post

Imagine offering something you know how to make or do: food at an event, handmade items, a lesson, or a small service. Choose a price, a cost for each sale, a one-time setup cost, and an expected number of sales. Estimates or made-up amounts are fine. Assume you only prepare what you sell.

Use your own example and answer all the numbered questions. Post your response in Canvas.

  1. What would you offer, and who might want it? State your price, cost per sale, setup cost, and expected sales.
  2. Calculate money collected = price × sales. Calculate total cost = setup cost + cost per sale × sales. Subtract cost from money collected to find profit. What does the result tell you?
  3. Would this feel worth doing? Explain one thing besides money that matters to you, such as time, enjoyment, affordability, or helping someone.
  4. Choose a smaller number of sales and calculate the profit again. Does that change your opinion of the idea? Why?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s example. Ask a question, suggest a small change, or connect their example to something you have noticed. Give them something to respond to.

You might start with:

  • “What would happen if you charged $1 less? Would you expect more sales?”
  • “Would you want to count ___ as a cost? It might matter because…”

Explain a little of your thinking so your classmate can build on it.
