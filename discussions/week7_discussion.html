Week 7 / Discussion

Would it be worth offering?

How do costs and revenue affect whether a small project is worthwhile?

The situation

Suppose a club sells handmade items for $8 each. Materials cost $3 per item, and the club pays a $60 setup fee. The club sells 20 items.

The money collected is revenue. Total cost combines the setup fee and the materials. Profit is revenue minus cost.

Your post

Calculate the result for this club, then consider how someone might judge the plan.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. For 20 sales, calculate revenue, total cost, and profit. Show your arithmetic.
  2. Would this be worth doing for the club? What might matter besides the profit shown by this model?
  3. Suppose only 10 items sell. How would that affect profit and your recommendation? Explain any other assumptions you would want to check.

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
