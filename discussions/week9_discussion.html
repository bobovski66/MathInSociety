Connections that matter

How can one connection change the routes through a network?

The situation

Consider four locations A, B, C, and D. The undirected connections are A–B, B–C, C–D, and B–D.

A route is a sequence of connected locations. Its length counts connections traveled.

Your post

Compare routes from A to D, then consider what connections mean in a real setting.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Find a shortest route from A to D and count its edges. Now remove B–D and find the new shortest route.
  2. Imagine these are roads, communication links, or contacts in a group. Why might the changed route matter?
  3. Suggest one new link that would improve the network for some purpose. What real-world detail would you need before choosing it?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
