Module 9 Discussion — Local Connections, Collective Effects

Use network structure to explain how a local change can affect a larger group.

Prompt

A campus alert network links students, staff, and service locations. Some nodes have many connections; some have only one route into the network.

  1. Give a real or imagined network and identify its nodes and edges.
  2. Which node has high degree, and what can that tell us?
  3. Identify a path or possible bottleneck. What happens if one link fails?
  4. Propose an intervention that improves reach or resilience, and explain a possible tradeoff.

Post and respond

Include a small edge list or clear diagram, then support your claim with degree or path reasoning. Reply to one classmate by testing whether their proposed intervention creates an alternate path or changes who can be reached.
