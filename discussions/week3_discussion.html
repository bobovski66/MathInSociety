Week 3 / Discussion

What does an average miss?

How much can an average tell us about a set of measurements?

The situation

Four trips take 20, 25, 25, and 30 minutes. The times describe the same journey on different days.

A mean summarizes the center of these observations. A range describes the difference between the longest and shortest trips.

Your post

Calculate the summaries for these four trips and consider how they might affect a decision.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Find the mean and range of the four travel times. Show your arithmetic and include units.
  2. Suppose you need to arrive at an appointment on time. How useful would the mean be, and which other value might matter?
  3. Imagine a fifth trip takes 50 minutes. How might this change your interpretation of the route?

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
