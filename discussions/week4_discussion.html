How close is close enough?

When is an estimate precise enough to be useful?

The situation

A sample gives an estimated average waiting time of 12 minutes, with a reported margin of 3 minutes.

Subtracting and adding the margin creates an interval of plausible values for the population mean under the study's method.

Your post

Use the reported estimate and margin to interpret a possible decision.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Calculate the lower and upper endpoints of the interval. Include units.
  2. Imagine you are planning a visit where waiting time matters. Would this estimate be useful? What would you want to know about how it was collected?
  3. Does the interval describe every individual's wait? Explain the difference between a population average and one person's experience.

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
