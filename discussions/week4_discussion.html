The bottling problem

A beverage company uses an automated machine intended to fill bottles to an average of 500 mL. Historical process data indicate that individual fill amounts have standard deviation about 8 mL. For this exercise, treat individual volumes as approximately normal.

A technician finds one bottle containing 516 mL. How unusual is this individual bottle compared with the stated bottle-to-bottle model?

The technician also takes a separate sample of 64 bottles, with mean 503 mL and reported margin of error 2 mL. What does that sample suggest about the process mean?

DataQuestion
One bottle: 516 mL; reference mean 500; SD 8Locate an individual measurement
Separate sample: n = 64; mean 503; margin 2Construct an interval for a population mean

The margin is supplied. Construct the sample interval from 503 ± 2 rather than applying the individual standard deviation as the interval’s margin. The separate sample and the historical model play different roles.

Initial post

  1. Find the z-score of the 516 mL bottle.
  2. Use the Empirical Rule to approximate the percentage of individual bottles at least this far above the historical mean.
  3. Write the confidence interval determined by 503 ± 2 mL.
  4. Decide whether the process target 500 mL lies inside that interval.
  5. Explain why the z-score and the confidence interval answer different questions.

Replies

Read a classmate’s interpretation as well as their arithmetic. Identify a sentence that clearly distinguishes an individual measurement from an estimate of a population mean, or propose more precise wording where they are mixed together.

A useful reply checks a calculation, compares a method or assumption, or asks a specific mathematical question. For example: “Which population quantity does your interval estimate?” or “Why did you divide the outside percentage by two?”

Before posting

  • I used the individual SD for the z-score and the supplied margin for the sample interval.
  • I identified an upper tail, rather than both tails together.
  • I kept volume and interval endpoints in mL.
  • I stated what the interval estimates and avoided a claim about every bottle.
  • I explained the role of the historical model, the separate sample, and the stated assumptions.
