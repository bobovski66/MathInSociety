Making room for what matters

How do we choose among plans when resources are limited?

The situation

A club has 3 hours and $20 for an afternoon. Activity A takes 2 hours and costs $12. Activity B takes 1 hour and costs $9.

A plan is feasible when it fits both limits. Consider doing either activity or both.

Your post

Use the time and money limits above to compare possible plans.

Post your answers in Canvas.

  1. Calculate the total time and cost of doing both activities. Does that plan meet each limit? Show the comparisons.
  2. Would you choose A, B, or a modified combination? Describe what you would value most in this situation.
  3. If the club could gain either one extra hour or $2 more, which would expand its options? Explain how you know.

Reply to a classmate

Read a classmate’s post. Ask a question, offer another consideration, or explain how a changed assumption might affect the result.
