01 / Send

Information theory asks what can be communicated.

How many possible messages are there? How uncertain are they? What happens when a signal travels through noise? How can errors be detected or corrected?

02 / Respond

Cybernetics asks what information makes a system do.

What does a sensor measure? How does a controller choose an action? Can it stabilize a system, adapt to disturbances—or begin to oscillate?

About this page

Compiled for MATH& 107 · Math in Society. This is a selected teaching timeline, not an exhaustive history. Entries simplify advanced work to emphasize the ideas and questions that students can meaningfully explore. The final entry describes ongoing research as of October 2026, not a single dated invention.

The early probability and entropy developments predate the names information theory and cybernetics; they are included as mathematical precursors, not as instances of those later disciplines.
