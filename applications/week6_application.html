Week 6 Application — Credit-Card Repayment

Compare two fixed monthly payment plans on the same balance and interest rate.

How to complete this application

  1. Compute the first month of each plan by separating interest from principal reduction.
  2. Run the same recurrence to payoff and compare months and total interest, not only the monthly payment.
  3. Use the common first-month interest charge to explain why later balances—not the APR—create the difference.

Math model

With monthly rate \(i=r/12\), interest is \(iB\) and the new balance is \(B(1+i)-M\), where \(M\) is the payment.

Context

An $8,000 balance at 18% APR

A borrower has an $8,000 credit-card balance. The card charges a fixed 18% annual percentage rate (APR), compounded monthly. Assume no new purchases, fees, or rate changes. Interest is added once each month, then the payment is made.

monthly rate = 0.18/12 = 0.015 = 1.5%
new balance = old balance(1.015) − payment

Compare Plan A: $160 per month with Plan B: $300 per month. The final payment is allowed to be smaller than the regular payment.
