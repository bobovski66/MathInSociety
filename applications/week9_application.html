Week 9 Application — Least-Cost Supplement Mix

Use minimum nutrient requirements to build and solve a linear program.

How to complete this application

  1. Write both minimum nutrient requirements as inequalities and keep the objective—cost—separate.
  2. Find the boundary intersection and axis vertices, then compare their costs.
  3. After finding the continuous optimum, check nearby feasible whole-serving choices because the practical restriction changes the allowable set.

Math model

Minimum requirements lead to \(\ge\) constraints. The continuous linear-programming optimum occurs at a feasible vertex; integer restrictions require a second check of nearby integer points.

Scenario

Daily iron and zinc supplement

A nutrition program can mix two powdered supplements. Fractional servings are allowed because the powders are measured by weight.
