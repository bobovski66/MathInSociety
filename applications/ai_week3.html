AI Mathematics 3 — Attention as a Weighted Average

A central transformer operation can be introduced with the same weighted-mean mathematics used in basic statistics.

Why this belongs in Math in Society

Attention lets one part of the text combine information from other parts.

When a transformer processes a token, it computes numerical attention weights for other relevant tokens. After normalization, those weights are nonnegative and add to 1. The model then forms a weighted combination of numerical information carried by those tokens.

This is mathematically close to a weighted mean: large weights contribute more, small weights contribute less. The values being averaged in a real transformer are vectors rather than single numbers, but each coordinate is combined by the same weighted-sum idea.

What is simplified here? An attention weight is not automatically a complete explanation of what the model 'thought.' Real transformers have many attention heads and layers, and they combine vectors with learned transformations before and after attention.
