AI Mathematics 2 — Prediction Is a Probability Distribution

Next-token probabilities, sampling, expected counts, and why temperature changes the feel of generated text.

Why this belongs in Math in Society

An LLM does not normally produce one inevitable next word.

After processing the current context, a language model assigns a numerical score to every possible next token. Those scores are transformed into a probability distribution. The probabilities are nonnegative and add to 1.

Generation can then sample from that distribution. A token with probability 0.50 is more likely than a token with probability 0.10, but the 0.10 token is still possible. This is one reason two responses to the same prompt can differ.

What is simplified here? Modern models score enormous vocabularies, and the probabilities depend on all of the preceding context. Temperature is implemented by transforming model scores before probabilities are computed; here we compare already-transformed toy distributions.
