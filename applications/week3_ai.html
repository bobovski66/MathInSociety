Module 3: AI Application — Attention as a Weighted Average

How a model can combine information from several earlier tokens.

Why this page exists

Mathematics gives us a way into AI

Modern language models use a mechanism called attention. The real calculation uses vectors and matrix multiplication, but its statistical heart can be introduced with mathematics you already know: a weighted average.

Important: the examples on this page are intentionally tiny models. Real LLMs use huge vectors, many layers, and billions of learned numerical parameters. We simplify the scale while keeping the mathematical structure recognizable.

Connection to this module

The mathematics you already know

Module 3 is about center and spread. Attention produces weighted combinations in which some earlier pieces of the sentence count more than others.

AI vocabulary

Four ideas to carry with you

attention weightA nonnegative number indicating how strongly one token contributes to a calculation.
weighted averageAn average in which different values receive different amounts of weight.
contextThe earlier tokens available to the model when it predicts the next one.
featureA numerical coordinate used by the model to represent some aspect of a token.
Worked example

Follow the numbers

Suppose four earlier tokens have a simplified numerical feature \[ 2,quad 5,quad 8,quad 10 \] and the model assigns attention weights \[ 0.10,quad0.20,quad0.60,quad0.10. \] The attention output for this one feature is \[ 0.10(2)+0.20(5)+0.60(8)+0.10(10)=7. \] The value 8 matters most because it receives the largest weight.
Investigation

Try the mathematics yourself

1. Do the attention weights 0.10, 0.20, 0.60, 0.10 form a probability-style distribution?

2. Compute 0.10(2)+0.20(5)+0.60(8)+0.10(10).

3. If all four attention weights were 0.25, what would the weighted average of 2, 5, 8, 10 be?

4. Which token contributes most when its attention weight is 0.60?

What to remember

AI idea of the week

Attention is not “the model looking at a word” in a human sense. Mathematically, it is a way of computing context-dependent weighted combinations. That modest idea, repeated at enormous scale, is one of the central mechanisms of transformer models.

You do not need to know every engineering detail of an LLM to reason mathematically about what it is doing. We will keep adding one mathematical layer at a time.
