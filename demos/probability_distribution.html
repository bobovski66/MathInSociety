Frequency → Probability Explorer

Change observed frequencies and watch them become a relative-frequency distribution.

How to use this explorer

Change one count at a time. First read the raw frequency, then its relative frequency, and finally check that all relative frequencies still total 1. The point is to see how a sample becomes an empirical probability distribution.
