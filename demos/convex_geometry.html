Try these three investigations

  1. Start with the reading’s snack-pack model and test (5,3). Which limit fails? Explain why a point can lie on one boundary and still be infeasible.
  2. Keep the packing limit at 8. Raise the ingredient limit from 12 to 16, then to 20. Which limit eventually stops the region from expanding?
  3. Choose the lab model. Find the intercepts of each boundary, then compare them with the actual corner table. Explain why not every intercept becomes a feasible vertex.
